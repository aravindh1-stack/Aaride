Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Aaride compliance and telemetry.

Can drivers modify or replace uploaded document files?

No. In adherence to enterprise statutory compliance, only authorized administrators can upload physical files to the Supabase Storage bucket. Drivers have full view access and can update editable metadata like policy numbers and expiry dates.

How does the expiry countdown alert work?

Our background telemetry evaluates all document expiration dates daily. When an RC Book, License, or Insurance policy is within 30 days of expiring, it automatically flags as amber on both the driver and fleet manager dashboards.

How does daily shift ledger profit calculation work?

When a driver records total gross fares and shift expenses (such as fuel and maintenance), the system deterministically calculates the net shift margin and logs an immutable ledger entry.
